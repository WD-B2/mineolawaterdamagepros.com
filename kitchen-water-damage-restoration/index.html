Kitchen Water Damage Restoration in Mineola, NY.

Leaks under sinks and behind dishwashers soak cabinets and flooring in a kitchen faster than most people expect. Here is what restoration involves.

Kitchen water damage restoration in Mineola, NY covers the cabinets, countertops, floors and walls affected by a leak under the sink, a failed dishwasher line, a refrigerator supply line or a pipe in the wall. Kitchens hold a lot of plumbing in a small space, and cabinets hide leaks until the base swells. Shut off the water under the sink or at the main valve and call if water is spreading.

The pattern is usually the same. A drip lands in a cabinet base, soaks the particleboard floor of the cabinet and spreads into the subfloor, and the first sign is a soft panel or a musty smell.

Reading the damage under a sink

What cabinet and floor materials do when wet
Material. What happens. Can it be saved?.
Solid wood. Swells, then may dry and recover. Often, with quick drying.
Plywood. Delaminates over time. Sometimes, if caught early.
Particleboard and MDF. Swells and crumbles. Usually not.
Laminate flooring. Edges swell and lift. Usually not.
Tile with a wet subfloor. Looks fine, subfloor stays wet. Subfloor needs drying and tile may need to be lifted.

Many kitchens in older Mineola homes were remodeled in stages, and the plumbing may be a mix of old and new pipes. Newer fittings under a new sink may sit on old supply lines, and the weakest point fails first.

How restoration is done

1

Shut off.

Close the water at the sink or main and unplug appliances if the area is dry.

2

Open up.

The crew removes the toe kick and the cabinet base where needed to reach wet material.

3

Dry.

Air movers and dehumidifiers run, with moisture readings checked daily.

4

Rebuild.

Cabinets, flooring and finishes are repaired or replaced once readings are dry.

What to pull out first

Empty the wet cabinet so air can reach the base. Move dishes, cleaning supplies and food out of the area, and set them on a dry counter. Pull the toe kick if it comes off easily, since water collects behind it. Do not run the dishwasher or use the sink until the leak is found.

Countertops are usually not the problem. Stone and solid surface tops sit on the cabinet frame and survive a wet base, but the cabinet below may have to come out, and the top has to be lifted to do it. Ask the crew whether the countertop can be saved before cabinets are removed.

Preventing the next kitchen leak

  • Replace rubber supply lines with braided stainless lines on the sink and dishwasher.
  • Check the shut-off valves under the sink and make sure they turn.
  • Put a small tray or leak sensor under the sink.
  • Look under the sink with a flashlight every few months for drips, stains and swelling.

These steps cost little and catch most small leaks before cabinets are damaged.

Kitchen water losses follow the IICRC S500 standard, which calls for measuring moisture under cabinets and flooring before closing anything back up.

Before we arrive, shut the valve under the sink, unplug small appliances if the outlet is dry and empty cabinet contents onto a dry surface.

Appliances and kitchens

The appliance, not the cabinet, is usually the source. The failure of a dishwasher or refrigerator line is covered on the appliance leak water damage page. A pipe that burst inside the wall behind the sink is covered under burst pipe water damage.

Wet floors beside the kitchen, including hardwood floor water damage repair, follow different rules than cabinets. Dishwasher leaks often reach the dining room floor through the subfloor.

Insurance and documentation

Sudden leaks are often covered by homeowners policies, and slow leaks often are not. Photograph the cabinet base, the supply lines and the floor before anything is removed. Keep receipts for emergency work. The New York State Department of Financial Services says to document losses before cleaning up.

Questions about kitchen water damage in Mineola

Can cabinets be saved after a leak?

Solid wood and some plywood can be dried if caught quickly. Particleboard and MDF usually swell and fail.

How can I find a hidden kitchen leak?

Look for a soft cabinet floor, a musty smell, a water meter that moves with everything off or a stain at the base of the wall.

Is it safe to cook while the kitchen is drying?

Drying equipment runs for days and the area around it may be restricted. The crew will explain what is safe.

Does the dishwasher leak void my insurance?

No. Sudden leaks are often covered, but gradual leaks often are not. Ask your insurer.

Who replaces the cabinets?

A contractor handles the rebuild, and permits may apply. Ask the Village of Mineola Building Department.
