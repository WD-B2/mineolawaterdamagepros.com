Water damage restoration in Mineola

Local help when water gets into the house

Water damage restoration in Mineola, NY is the removal of water, the drying of walls and floors and the repair of what the water ruined. We serve homes and businesses in the village and the nearby Nassau County towns. Dispatch is open every hour of every day, so a pipe that fails at 3 a.m. gets the same answer as one that fails at noon.

Mineola is the county seat of Nassau County, and its 11501 ZIP code covers older Cape Cods and colonials as well as apartment buildings and shops around the train station and Jericho Turnpike. We also take calls from Williston Park (11596), Carle Place (11514), Albertson (11507) and New Hyde Park (11040 and 11042). Our address is 540 Jericho Turnpike, Mineola, NY 11501.

Our crews work to the IICRC S500 standard for water damage and the S520 standard for mold. Those are published industry standards, and we name them so you know what the drying process is measured against.

What water damage restoration costs in Mineola

Price depends on the source of the water, how long it ran, how many rooms are wet and which materials are involved. A small supply line leak caught early costs far less than a basement that sat wet for days. A dispatcher can explain how scope drives price when you call (516) 845-9582.

Water damage questions in Mineola

Is emergency water damage service available at night in Mineola?

Yes. The line is open 24 hours a day, every day. Call (516) 845-9582, describe what is wet and follow the dispatcher's steps while a crew is arranged.

What should I do first when a pipe bursts?

Close the main water valve, or the fixture valve if you know where it is. Then switch off power to wet areas only if the panel is dry, lift valuables and call.

How long does drying take?

Most structures need several days of equipment, but it depends on the materials and how much water got in. Moisture readings decide when drying is done.

Does homeowners insurance cover water damage?

Often it covers sudden and accidental water damage but not flood or long-term seepage. Check your policy or ask your insurer, and photograph the damage first.

Which ZIP codes do you serve?

Mineola 11501, Williston Park 11596, Carle Place 11514, Albertson 11507 and New Hyde Park 11040 and 11042, plus nearby Nassau County addresses.

Do you handle mold?

Can you help commercial properties?

Yes. Shops, offices and multi-unit buildings can call the same line. The commercial page explains how losses are handled.
