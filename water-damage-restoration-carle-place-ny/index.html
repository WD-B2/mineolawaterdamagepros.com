Water damage restoration in Carle Place, NY is the removal of water, the drying of the structure and the rebuild after leaks, floods and storms in the hamlet. Carle Place is a census-designated place in the Town of North Hempstead with about 5,005 residents. Emergency dispatch is available around the clock.

Postwar houses and what their age means for water

In 1946 developer William J. Levitt bought 19 acres near the Carle Place train station and built about 600 low-cost houses, a prototype for the larger Levittown that began the following year. Houses of that era are small and plain, and kitchens, bathrooms and laundry often sit close together, so one leak can reach several rooms.

Homes built in the 1940s can still have some original pipe, old water heaters and older drain lines. If you are not sure what your home has, a plumber can tell you, and you should know where the main shut-off is before you need it.

If your Carle Place house is taking on water

If water is moving, shut the nearest valve, turn off power to wet areas only if it is safe and call. The line is open at night and on weekends, and a dispatcher will help you with the first steps.

Stop the source

Close the main valve or the fixture valve. If you cannot find it, say so when you call.

Protect the floor

Lift boxes, rugs and electronics off wet floors.

Keep clear

Stay away from water near outlets and keep pets out of drain water.

Record it

Photograph the water and the damage before cleanup begins.

Borders, neighbors and who to ask about permits

The hamlet takes its name from the Carle House, a 32-room home built by Silas Carle in the 1800s, and was once called Mineola Park. Old Country Road marks the southern edge and Carle Road the east. The border with Mineola follows the former Long Island Motor Parkway. Knowing the borders matters, because Carle Place also touches Westbury and Garden City, which have different villages and rules.

The hamlet is also a commercial and light industrial area. Water losses in retail and warehouse space follow the steps on the commercial water damage restoration page.
