Carpet water damage restoration in Mineola, NY covers wall-to-wall carpet, area rugs and carpet pad that have been soaked by a leak, a flood or a backup. Carpet absorbs water quickly and pad holds it longer, which makes speed important. If your carpet is wet, call, stop the source, lift furniture off the carpet and keep foot traffic off it.

Whether carpet is saved depends on the water, not the carpet. Clean water caught fast is a drying job, while contaminated water is a replacement job.

When carpet can be saved

Water category and carpet decisions
Water category. Carpet. Pad.
Category 1 (clean) within 24 to 48 hours. Usually dried in place. Often dried or replaced.
Category 2 (gray water). May be cleaned and dried if treated promptly. Usually replaced.
Category 3 (sewage, floodwater). Usually discarded. Discarded.
Long-standing water of any kind. Usually discarded. Discarded.

Pad is inexpensive compared with the cost of drying it, so it is often replaced even when the carpet is saved. The crew checks the subfloor beneath, because wet subfloor needs drying too.

How the carpet is dried

1

Extract.

Weighted extraction units pull water out of the carpet and pad.

2

Lift.

The carpet may be lifted at the edge and the pad removed so air can reach the subfloor.

3

Dry.

Air movers and dehumidifiers dry the carpet and subfloor.

4

Clean.

Carpet that is saved is cleaned and checked for odor.

What to do in the first hour

  1. Stop the source and shut off the water.
  2. Lift furniture off the wet carpet or put blocks under the legs.
  3. Remove small items and rugs and hang them to dry.
  4. Photograph the carpet and the damaged areas.
  5. Call the 24/7 line, and open windows only if humidity outside is lower.

Do not use a household vacuum on wet carpet, and do not walk on it more than necessary. Walking pushes water deeper into the pad and the subfloor.

Area rugs and carpet tiles

Area rugs can usually be taken out, cleaned and dried separately. Carpet tiles can be lifted and dried or replaced one at a time, which is why many offices use them. Wall-to-wall carpet is stretched over pad and tack strips, so it is lifted at the edge and rolled back to dry the pad and floor, then restretched if it is saved.

Wool and natural fiber rugs need specialist cleaning after a flood. Ask before you throw one out, because some can be saved.

The IICRC S500 standard guides the choice between drying carpet in place and removing it, and the water category decides which of the two is appropriate.

Wet carpet and mold

Mold grows on damp carpet backing, pad and subfloor. If you smell mustiness or see dark spots under a lifted edge, carpet may be hiding moisture. Mold over ten square feet falls under New York's licensing rules, explained on the mold remediation page.

Carpet in older and newer Mineola homes

Older homes often have hardwood under carpet, and a wet carpet may mean wet wood. Newer or renovated spaces may have carpet over concrete in basements, where moisture rises. If your carpet is in a basement, see basement water damage cleanup for how lower levels are handled.

Heavy furniture left on wet carpet stains it and traps moisture. Lift it onto blocks or aluminum foil if you can.

Questions about carpet water damage in Mineola

How fast do I need to act on wet carpet?

As fast as you can. Carpet that is dried within the first day or two has the best chance of being saved.

Can wet carpet pad be saved?

Usually it is replaced, because it takes a long time to dry and may hold odors.

Will drying remove the smell?

Drying helps. Contaminated water leaves odors that may require removal of the carpet.

Should I pull up the carpet myself?

If you are careful, lifting a corner to check the pad is reasonable. Leave larger removals to the crew so the carpet can be reinstalled.

Is carpet from a sewage backup salvageable?

Almost never. It is normally discarded.
