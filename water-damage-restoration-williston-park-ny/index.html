A village of 0.63 square miles

Its own village government

A neighbor of Mineola

The village borders Mineola, East Williston and the hamlets of Albertson, Herricks and Roslyn Heights.

Emergency water in Williston Park: the first minutes

Treat water as an emergency if it is moving across a floor, dripping through a ceiling or rising from a drain. Close the main valve if a pipe is the source, lift valuables off the floor and keep children away from water near outlets. Call the 24/7 line from a dry spot and describe where the water started.

The water problems homes here report

Williston Park was built as a planned community. Developer William Chatlos bought 195 acres to create an affordable place for New York City families moving to the suburbs, and the early housing dates to the first decades of the 1900s. Homes that old can still have older plumbing and finished lower levels, so supply line failures, drain backups and basement seepage are the problems to expect.

Tight lots matter too. The village peaked at 9,154 residents in 1970 and had 7,591 in the 2020 census, with homes close together. A leak in a bathroom or laundry often reaches a ceiling below, which is covered under ceiling water damage repair.

Before We Arrive in Williston Park

Shut the water off

Close the main valve, or the fixture valve if you know where it is.

Check the panel

Switch off power to the wet area only if the panel is dry and reachable.

Lift what you can

Get furniture, bags and electronics off wet floors.

Keep clear

Keep children and pets out of the water, especially drain water.

Take photos

Photograph the water, the source and the damage before anything moves.

Is emergency water removal available in Williston Park?

Yes. The dispatch line is open 24 hours a day, every day. Emergency water removal calls are taken at any hour, and a crew is sent as soon as one is available.
